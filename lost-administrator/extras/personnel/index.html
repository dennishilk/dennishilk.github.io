FILE · DH-16 Dennis Hilk The Author · Meta Record

FILE · DH-16 · META RECORD

Dennis Hilk

Position
Author · Builder · Probably Responsible

Affiliation
The Lost Administrator
dennishilk.com

Professional
Linux enthusiast, open-source developer, habitual system builder, and collector of projects that began as significantly smaller ideas.

Interested less in what a machine was intended to do than in understanding how it works — and what else it might do.

Known For
Asking “What if I...?” immediately before creating considerably more work for himself than the original question appeared to require.

Also known for installing Linux on things, taking systems apart, keeping old technology useful, and considering “because it works” a perfectly reasonable explanation.

Personal
Curious by default.

Has been opening, testing, repairing, rebuilding, documenting, and occasionally breaking things in order to understand them for most of his life.

The tools changed.

The question did not.

Archive Notes
The Lost Administrator did not begin as a plan to write a novel.

It began with another question:

“What if I built a realistic Debian workstation belonging to someone who had suddenly disappeared?”

The workstation needed files.

The files needed emails.

The emails needed people.

The people needed a reason to exist.

Obviously, that required a small story.

The small story required a chapter.

The chapter required another chapter.

Twenty-four chapters later, Michael Weber had a family, a company, an investigation, a legacy recovery system, a missing GMC Yukon, two household animals, and considerably more problems than originally planned.

At no point is there convincing evidence that this was the plan.

There is, however, considerable evidence that Dennis asked:

“What if I...?”
