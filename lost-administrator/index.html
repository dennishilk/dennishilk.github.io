An Interactive Story Experience

The Lost Administrator combines interactive exploration and storytelling.

Instead of playing a traditional game, visitors explore the working environment of an experienced Debian administrator.

Emails, tickets, scripts, photographs, notes and terminal history slowly reveal both an ordinary professional life and a much larger story hidden beneath it.

01 / STORY

Story

Meet Michael Weber, an experienced Debian system administrator. Visitors experience his world through the workstation where routine maintenance, long-running projects and personal history have accumulated over time.

02 / ENVIRONMENT

Interactive Workstation

The first version of Michael's Debian workstation is available to explore. It is a place to understand, not a challenge to beat.

OPEN WORKSTATION →

03 / DETAIL

World Building

The planned environment includes realistic projects, logs, emails, Git repositories, Bash history, notes, calendars, tickets and personal documents—small records that make a professional life feel lived in.

04 / APPROACH

Storytelling Philosophy

The mystery develops slowly. Believable people, ordinary routines and realistic technology come first; unexplained events emerge only after the working world has become familiar.

05 / PRIVACY

Browser Only

This fictional experience runs and is stored locally inside the browser. It is not a real shell: no commands execute on your machine, there is no backend or real Linux system, and no workstation network access. Every visitor receives an independent virtual workstation.
