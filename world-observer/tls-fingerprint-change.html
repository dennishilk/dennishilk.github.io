03 / EVIDENCE BOUNDARY

What the export can — and cannot — say

Observed fields, browser-derived summaries and unexported certificate or network context remain deliberately separate.

Observed

  • Current significant-event count, country count and significance flags.
  • Per-country sample size, completeness, TLS success, abort rate, ALPN presence and change score.
  • Exported TLS-version and cipher-class distributions.
  • Published significant-event history.

Derived

  • — current country profiles have TLS 1.3 as the complete exported version distribution.
  • — current country profiles have AES-256 as the complete exported cipher-class distribution.
  • Historical non-zero points, value changes and the current zero-run are counted in the browser.

Unknown

  • Certificate hashes/fingerprints, subject, issuer, SANs, expiry and endpoint identities are not exported here.
  • Provider, route, interception, policy or operational causes are not established by these fields.
  • The sample set must not be treated as a country-wide TLS census or user-experience measurement.
04 / PROVENANCE

Method & source boundary

This presentation adds no external enrichment and does not reconstruct data that the public export does not contain.

Methodology

The published observer defines tls_change_score from changes in TLS-version distribution, cipher-class distribution, positive handshake-abort-rate change and absolute ALPN-presence-rate change, with components clipped to the 0–1 range. The export currently uses a —σ significance multiplier. The visual “surface” on this page represents those exported profile fields; it is not a certificate fingerprint.
