03 / EARTH IS NOT A CLOCK

THE ROTATION REFERENCE DRIFTS IRREGULARLY

Atomic time is regular. UT1 tracks Earth’s rotational angle, which varies with atmosphere, oceans, mass redistribution and long-term rotational change.

04 / TIME MACHINE

UTC HAS A HISTORY OF ADJUSTMENTS
