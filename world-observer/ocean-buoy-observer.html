Source & methodology

Observations come from the NOAA National Data Buoy Center latest-observations feed. Coverage is predominantly United States waters and NDBC partner stations; this is not a complete worldwide buoy inventory.

The production export is generated hourly. Its generation time and each station’s own observation time are distinct. Unavailable measurements remain missing rather than becoming zero; the backend handles stale or malformed records. Full recent snapshots are retained for seven days, while compact aggregate history is retained separately.
