04 / EVIDENCE BOUNDARY

What the export can — and cannot — say

Raw group fields, transparent browser arithmetic and unavailable context stay deliberately separate.

Observed

  • Per-group GP record count and unique NORAD_CAT_ID count.
  • Exported GP EPOCH values and INCLINATION fields.
  • Which selected group queries completed or were not attempted after a source error.
  • The observer's own published daily group-count memory.

Derived

  • Oldest/newest epoch among records in each selected group.
  • Median epoch age and mean inclination per group.
  • Relative freshness bars within the current selected-group export.
  • Published history length and first/latest local observation dates.

Unknown

  • Real-time satellite positions, trajectories, visibility from a location or future passes.
  • Operational health, ownership meaning, mission status or service availability.
  • A global number of active satellites: selected groups may overlap and are not summed.
  • Objects or classifications outside the six explicitly queried CelesTrak groups.
05 / PROVENANCE

Method & source boundary

The browser reads only the locally published World Observer export. CelesTrak is contacted by the server-side collector, not by this page.

Methodology

The collector requests six explicit CelesTrak Current GP groups in OMM-keyword CSV: STATIONS, STARLINK, ONEWEB, GPS-OPS, GALILEO and CUBESAT. It caches one result per UTC day and performs no retries. On the first source error it stops the remaining queries for that run. No orbit propagation is performed.
