Reading the observations

Constant motion

Earthquakes occur constantly around the planet. Most are small and may never be felt.

Magnitude

Magnitude is logarithmic: each whole-number step represents a much larger release of energy.

Depth

Depth describes where rupture begins below the surface and helps give an observation its geological context.

Observe, don't speculate

This exhibit describes recorded events. Earthquake observations are not earthquake predictions.
