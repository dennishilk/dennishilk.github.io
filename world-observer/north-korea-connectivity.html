03 / EVIDENCE BOUNDARY

What the export can — and cannot — say

Published counts, simple numeric derivations and non-exported network details remain separate.

Observed

  • Current published TCP probe count.
  • Current published ICMP probe count.
  • Published TCP count history and observation dates.

Derived

  • Numeric history coverage is calculated from the public series.
  • Distinct TCP count values are counted from numeric points.
  • Consecutive TCP count changes are calculated from the public series.

Unknown

  • Probe destinations, responders and per-probe success or failure.
  • Route topology, providers, ASNs, RTT and packet loss.
  • The cause, intent or broader meaning of any future change.
04 / PROVENANCE

Method & source boundary

This observer-specific view adds no external enrichment.

Methodology

The current view reads the published TCP probe count from layers.tcp.probe_count and the published ICMP probe count from the dashboard secondary metrics. One visual cell is drawn per exported count unit. The cells are presentation marks, not hosts, packets, replies or paths. History statistics are calculated only from the published TCP probe-count series.
