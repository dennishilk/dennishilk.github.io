Your loot. Your rules.

A Custom WoW Item Creator for the entire community: invent stats, effects and set bonuses, or let the Funny WoW Item Generator do the rolling. Weapons, armor, rings, consumables and even Linux jokes belong here. Create your own WoW tooltip and share a little loot-shaped nonsense.

“Copy image” puts a real PNG on your clipboard when supported by your browser. You can also download the image. Favorites and custom items are saved in this browser only when you choose to save them. Delete them at any time in “My collection”.
