Popular conversions

Convert JPG, PNG and WebP directly in your browser

One converter handles the common format changes without creating an upload copy of your image on a server.

JPG / JPEG to WebP

Choose a JPG or JPEG image, select WebP and adjust the quality slider if needed. You can also set maximum dimensions to resize the image while converting it.

PNG to WebP

Convert PNG images to WebP locally. WebP can keep transparent pixels, while the quality control lets you balance visual quality and file size.

WebP to JPG or PNG

Open a WebP image and convert it back to JPG/JPEG or PNG. PNG can keep transparency; JPEG has no alpha channel, so transparent areas become white.

How to convert an image in 3 steps

  1. Choose your images. Drop in JPG/JPEG, PNG, WebP or browser-supported AVIF files.
  2. Choose the output. Select WebP, JPG/JPEG or PNG, then optionally set quality and maximum dimensions.
  3. Convert and download. Processing happens locally on your device; download each result or use Download all.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert JPG or JPEG to WebP?

Choose one or more JPG or JPEG images, select WebP as the output format, adjust quality or maximum dimensions if needed, and start the local conversion. The images are not uploaded.

How do I convert PNG to WebP without uploading it?

Choose the PNG file and select WebP as the output format. Conversion runs in your browser on your device. Transparent pixels can remain transparent in WebP output.

Can I convert WebP to JPG or PNG?

Yes. Choose a WebP image as input and select JPG/JPEG or PNG as the output. When converting to JPEG, transparent areas are flattened onto a white background.

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. Image decoding, resizing and conversion happen locally in your browser. The converter does not upload your selected image files.

Which image formats can I convert?

The tool accepts JPG/JPEG, PNG and WebP. AVIF input also works when the browser can decode AVIF. Output is WebP, JPG/JPEG or PNG when supported by the browser.

Can I convert several images at once?

Yes. You can add multiple images, convert them sequentially on your device and download the results individually or with the Download all button.

What happens to EXIF, GPS metadata and transparency?

The image is redrawn and re-encoded, so original EXIF and GPS metadata is generally not copied. PNG and WebP output can keep transparent pixels; JPEG output uses a white background.
