How does the local experiment work?

wllama runs a quantized Qwen model on your device in a worker. You choose when to download it from this website. Where supported, only model files are cached in origin-private browser storage; the browser can evict them. Without storage, loading is session-only. No chat history is stored or uploaded. Hardware support and answer quality vary; CPU mode can be slow. Only a small recent part of the conversation fits into memory. The experimental WebGPU path may fall back to CPU. A locally reviewed Platt mode and a daily word collection are future work.
