THE PHYSICS OF A LITTLE LIGHT

How it works 🔬

448 or 666 individual mirrors, one light source and the law of reflection.

  1. 01 — One light source

    One point source illuminates the array of independently aimed mirrors.

  2. 02 — Mirror orientation

    Each facet gets a calculated surface normal, not a painted pixel.

  3. 03 — The reflection law

    The incoming and outgoing rays have equal angles to the surface normal: r = i − 2(i · n)n.

  4. 04 — The projection

    The rays intersect the wall, building a motif from individual spots.

  5. 05 — A new message

    Each message scene recalculates all mirror normals, then animates their physical reorientation.

  6. 06 — Export and sharing

    The browser turns those calculated projections into GIF frames locally, with no upload.
