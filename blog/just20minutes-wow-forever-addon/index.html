Just 20 Minutes: A friendly playtime timer for Azeroth

Sometimes I plan to spend just 20 minutes in Azeroth – and suddenly I am off on a much longer adventure. So I made an addon that gently reminds me how much time I intended to spend playing. It is a tool for choice, not control.

Why I built it

On Sunday I was pretty frustrated by a small World of Warcraft video-editing task. Instead, I spent some time polishing Just 20 Minutes. A simple countdown has grown into a tidy interface with a widget, minimap button, six settings pages and friendly reminders. Today I captured the first genuine in-game views of its interface in WoW Forever on my Arch Linux machine.

Enjoy Azeroth. Remember real life.

Features and interface

What version 1.0.4 includes

  • Choose your timePick 10, 15, 20, 30, 45, 60, 90 or 120 minutes, or enter any whole number from 1 to 600.
  • Stay awareMoveable widget with progress bar, adjustable scale and opacity, compact mode and optional text status.
  • Minimap shortcutSmall pocket-watch button: left-click opens the timer, right-click opens Settings; move it around the minimap.
  • Gentle remindersClassic 10/5/2-minute and expiry alerts, plus adaptive warnings for shorter 1–5-minute sessions.
  • Your choiceStart, pause, resume, stop, reset and extend by 5/10/15 or custom minutes – no enforced stopping.
  • Six settings pagesGeneral, Appearance, Notifications, Experience, Profiles and About; full English and German support.

Real WoW Forever screenshots

All ten images were captured in the actual WoW Forever client on 23 November 2026 (Arch Linux). Select an image to view the larger WebP file.

The design aims to be calm: dark panels, warm gold accents and clear status changes. An idle widget says “Ready for Azeroth”; running and expiring sessions update their appearance. Settings pages scroll properly, and even 1–5-minute test sessions receive meaningful reminders instead of waiting for a ten-minute threshold.

Privacy and limitations

Not parental control: no tracking and no forced logout

Preferences stay in World of Warcraft's local SavedVariables. The addon does not send telemetry, contact a network service or monitor other players. Family Mode changes the wording of reminders but is not a technical child-safety restriction. The addon never forces a logout or takes over character actions.

My screenshots show version 1.0.4 in the actual game client. The full manual regression checklist for every edge case is still pending; automated checks are documented in the repository. /reload, rapid relogging and crashes have inherent limitations due to WoW's SavedVariables system.

Installation and download

How to start a timer

  1. Extract the ZIP into World of Warcraft/_forever_/Interface/AddOns/, leaving exactly one Just20Minutes folder.
  2. Restart WoW or use /reload. Open the addon from the minimap or type /j20.
  3. Pick a duration, press Start and enjoy Azeroth. Automatic start on login is disabled by default and must be enabled explicitly.
  4. If you want to play longer, extend your session yourself. Reminders are suggestions, not a lockout.
