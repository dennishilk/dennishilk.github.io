      Technical context

      Open a topic when you want the engineering detail behind the current procedure.

      ATX & power

      ATX standardizes board dimensions, mounting, and power distribution. The 24-pin feeds the board; a separately keyed EPS cable feeds CPU regulators.

      Memory & PCI Express

      Two modules use this board’s A2/B2 recommendation for dual-channel operation. PCIe provides lanes to the graphics card and NVMe storage.

      M.2 & airflow

      M.2 names a physical form factor; this drive uses the NVMe protocol over PCIe. Controlled front-to-rear flow moves heat out of the chassis.

      POST & UEFI

      POST checks essential hardware before an operating system starts. UEFI firmware enumerates devices and selects a boot target.
