museum@linux:~$ PERMISSIONS & USERS LAB

ASK WHY ACCESS WAS DENIED

Permissions are a way of describing who may read, write, or run something. The point is not to blindly prefix a command with sudo, but to understand the denial first.

WHAT YOU WILL LEARN

  • Inspect identity and membership with whoami, id, users, and groups.
  • Read ls -l: owner, group, other, and the rwx permission model.
  • Understand the concepts behind chmod, root, and sudo.

MISSION PREVIEW

A FICTIONAL SCRIPT WILL NOT RUN.
INSPECT WHY.
FIX ONLY THE NECESSARY PERMISSION.

FUTURE INTERACTION

The lab will model users and permissions without real privilege escalation, sudo access, or changes to a visitor’s computer.

SAFETY / BROWSER-ONLY

This lab is planned information only. It does not include an interactive terminal yet, and it will never run real shell commands or access your files, processes, services, or operating system.
