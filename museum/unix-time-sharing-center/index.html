Why Time Sharing?

One central host served many simultaneous terminal sessions. UNIX coordinated accounts, permissions, processes, files, communication, and devices so finite resources could be shared responsibly.

Chesapeake Signal Tech maintains cs-vax1 because its specialized workflows remain stable, dependable, and well documented.
