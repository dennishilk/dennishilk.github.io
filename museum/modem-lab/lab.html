Why the handset? The coupler uses acoustic interfaces aligned with the telephone handset's speaker and microphone, allowing computer data signals to travel through an ordinary voice telephone connection.

What is a carrier? A carrier is a signal used by communicating modems/couplers to establish and maintain a data link.

Idealized maximum
~30 CHARACTERS / SECOND

This is an educational simplification. Historical systems used different character widths, parity, and framing conventions. The exhibit chooses 300 BIT/S plus simulated 8N1 so the relationship between line bits and visible characters is understandable. Baud measures symbols per second and is not universally identical to bit/s.
