Why the handset? The coupler uses acoustic interfaces aligned with the telephone handset's speaker and microphone, allowing computer data signals to travel through an ordinary voice telephone connection.

What is a carrier? A carrier is a signal used by communicating modems/couplers to establish and maintain a data link. This exhibit uses 300 BIT/S for the line rate; baud means symbols per second and is not universally identical to bits per second.
