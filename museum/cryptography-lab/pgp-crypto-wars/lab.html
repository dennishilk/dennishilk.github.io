CRYPTO WARS TIMELINE

  1. 1991 PGP is released to ordinary personal computers.
  2. 1993–96 Zimmermann investigation and software/export controversy sharpen debate.
  3. 1990s Source publication and export rules become prominent public questions.
  4. 2000 U.S. controls are broadly relaxed for many mass-market encryption exports; policy debates continue.

Strong cryptography became a question of privacy, civil liberties, commerce, and government policy—not just mathematics.
