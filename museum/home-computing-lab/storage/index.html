From floppy disks to networked storage

Capacity is a changing feeling

Storage is measured in numbers, but experienced as choices. A 100 MB Zip disk held roughly 69 times the capacity of a 1.44 MB 3.5-inch floppy disk. That difference made large files, backups and transfers feel newly practical—even though it is tiny beside current storage.

  1. 3.5-inch floppy disk1.44 MB · compact transfer and software distribution.
  2. Iomega Zip100 MB · a dramatic jump for personal removable storage.
  3. CD-R650–700 MB · distribution and archival copies on optical media.
  4. Larger local storageMore room for applications, media and personal files.
  5. USB storagePortable capacity without a dedicated legacy drive.
  6. Network storageFiles and backups served across a local network.
  7. Online storage conceptsRemote services add convenience, synchronization and new questions of access and ownership.

What media taught

Before fast, always-on networks, moving files often meant carrying an object. Media capacity influenced backup plans, software distribution and which documents or games could travel. The functioning Zip drive in this environment will receive a future photograph and close technical detail.
