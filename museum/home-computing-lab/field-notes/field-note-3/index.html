FIELD NOTE #003 · DOCUMENTED 2026

THE MISSING CAPACITOR

A CPU cooler slipped during installation and tore a capacitor completely off this motherboard. What followed was not a repair, but an unexpected period of continued daily use.

Situation

This motherboard originally lived in an earlier desktop, before the current Cthulhu build. During CPU cooler installation, the cooler unexpectedly slipped out of my hand and struck the motherboard near the CPU socket.

A capacitor was torn completely off the board. At that moment, I assumed the motherboard was probably destroyed.

What Happened

The capacitor could not simply be soldered back. The two remaining wire ends stayed inside the motherboard. Without removing those remnants, reinstalling the original capacitor was not realistically possible.

Instead of attempting further repairs, I decided to assemble the computer anyway and simply see what would happen.

To my surprise, the system booted normally. Even more surprisingly, it continued to work perfectly.

No crashes. No instability that I could ever attribute to the missing capacitor. No obvious problems.

The motherboard remained in daily use for another two to three years until it was eventually replaced by newer hardware.

I never found out exactly what that capacitor was responsible for. The exact function of this specific missing component remains unknown.

Solution

No repair was performed. The motherboard simply remained in service. Years later, after upgrading to newer hardware, it was retired.

Lesson

Sometimes a computer survives damage that looks catastrophic.

That does not mean the damage was harmless. It simply means this particular motherboard continued to operate despite losing one component.

Today

The motherboard now hangs on my wall. Not because it is valuable, but because it reminds me to slow down whenever I work on hardware.

It became a permanent reminder that careful handling is usually faster than rushing.
