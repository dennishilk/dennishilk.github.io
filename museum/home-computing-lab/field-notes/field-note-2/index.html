FIELD NOTE #002 · DOCUMENTED 2026

THE M.2 SCREW

Installing an M.2 SSD normally takes only a few minutes. Unless the smallest part of the entire installation decides to explore the inside of the computer case.

Situation

The system was open for a routine M.2 SSD installation.

The SSD itself was installed without difficulty.

Only the tiny mounting screw still had to be secured.

The opening beneath the SSD appeared harmless during installation, but it led directly toward the power supply compartment.

What Happened

While positioning the mounting screw it slipped from the screwdriver.

Instead of landing somewhere inside the case, it passed perfectly through the opening beneath the motherboard.

The screw disappeared directly into the power supply compartment.

Several minutes of searching inside the main compartment followed before the realization arrived:

The screw had never remained inside the visible part of the case.

Solution

The power supply compartment was opened and the missing screw was recovered.

The SSD was finally secured using the same screw.

The installation itself had taken only a few minutes.

Recovering the screw took considerably longer.

Lesson

Tiny screws obey gravity with remarkable precision.
