FIELD NOTES & ARTIFACTS FROM THE LAB

PLANNED · GROWING

Behind every computer is a collection of small victories, unexpected failures, improvised repairs and ideas that somehow worked anyway. This archive documents genuine moments from the author’s own computing journey.

Nothing here is fictional. Every future Field Note must be based on a real event, and every Artifact must be a real object with personal and technical history. This is not a collection of perfect tutorials, marketing language or polished success stories; it records what building, repairing, experimenting and learning with computers actually feels like.

WORKSHOP RECORDS · AUTHENTIC EVENTS ONLY

FIELD NOTES

Field Notes will be short workshop stories recording a title, a date when known, the starting situation, what happened, the solution and the lesson, observation or punchline. Some may be funny, frustrating or unexpectedly educational.
