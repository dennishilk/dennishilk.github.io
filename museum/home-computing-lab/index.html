The Lab Today

COLLECTION HCL-2026
STATUS: ACTIVE / EVOLVING

This is a record of a working room, not an idealized hardware display. The systems documented here are used for daily Linux computing, software development, the World Observer, the Computer Museum, gaming, storage, networking and experiments with older computers.

Objects enter this archive because they performed a real task, changed a workflow or preserve evidence of an earlier stage of the lab. Current, retired and still-undocumented equipment are kept distinct. Unknown dates and motivations remain explicitly unrecorded rather than being reconstructed from assumption.

How this collection is documented

01 · IDENTIFY

Record maker, model, specification, condition and physical relationship to the rest of the lab.

02 · EXPLAIN

Record the problem an object solved, why it was selected and which project or constraint shaped the decision.

03 · PRESERVE CHANGE

Keep installation, retirement, predecessor and successor information so replacement does not erase the earlier system.

A blank field is evidence that research remains to be done. It is not an invitation to invent a convenient story.
