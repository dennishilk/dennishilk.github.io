REAL STORIES · REAL HARDWARE · REAL PROJECTS

FIELD NOTES & ARTIFACTS FROM THE LAB

PLANNED · GROWING

Behind every computer is a collection of small victories, unexpected failures, improvised repairs and ideas that somehow worked anyway. This section documents genuine moments from the author’s own computing journey.

Nothing here is fictional. Every future Field Note will be based on a real event, and every Artifact will be a real object that became part of that story. Rather than perfect tutorials, marketing language or polished success stories, this archive will show what building, repairing, experimenting and learning with computers actually feels like.

WORKSHOP RECORDS · AUTHENTIC EVENTS ONLY

Field Notes will be short workshop stories recording a title, a date when known, the starting situation, what happened, the solution and the lesson, observation or punchline. Some may be funny, frustrating or unexpectedly educational.
