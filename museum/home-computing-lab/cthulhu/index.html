Platform decision record

Why the Ryzen 7 5800X3D was chosen

World of Warcraft Classic is strongly CPU-dependent, and the Ryzen 7 5800X3D was selected primarily for that workload. A Gigabyte B550 Vision D was available at a good price, while moving to DDR5 was not attractive at prevailing prices. Staying with AM4 was therefore the sensible platform choice.

Configuration history

Hardware evolution

  1. EARLY CTHULHU CONFIGURATIONIntel Core i7-7700K · MSI GeForce GTX 970 Gaming 4GThe GTX 970 already served in the preceding system. It performed especially well in the author’s preferred games, so it remained in service into early Cthulhu.
  2. RYZEN PLATFORM STAGERyzen 7 5800X3D · Gigabyte GeForce RTX 3060 Ti Vision OC WhiteThe white RTX 3060 Ti was Cthulhu’s primary NVIDIA card after the Ryzen platform transition.
  3. TEMPORARY EVALUATION HARDWAREASUS Strix Radeon R9 380Purchased for €20 as a low-risk test of AMD graphics compatibility in the author’s preferred, self-configured Linux environments—not as a normal primary upgrade.
  4. CURRENT PRIMARY GRAPHICSPowerColor Radeon RX 9060 XT Hellhound Spectral WhiteThe successful AMD evaluation supported this later move. The card also matched the intended white workstation design; exact transition date remains undocumented.

Graphics transition evidence

From NVIDIA to AMD

The white Gigabyte GeForce RTX 3060 Ti Vision OC generally worked in preconfigured distributions such as CachyOS and Linux Mint, but those were not the author’s preferred operating environments. Occasional Linux driver or compatibility issues occurred on self-configured systems. The €20 R9 380 evaluation provided a low-risk compatibility test before committing to the later primary AMD card.

Storage evidence

Preserved Operating-System Drives

Samsung 970 EVO 250 GB

Last system drive before the Intel Core i7-to-AMD Ryzen transition. It preserves a Windows 10 and Debian dual-boot installation and represents the final dual-boot stage before the first Linux-only primary SSD.

PRESERVED IN LAST OPERATIONAL STATE

Corsair MP600 Gen4 PCIe

First primary SSD after the move to the Ryzen 7 5800X3D, and the first primary system drive in this sequence without Windows. It preserves Gentoo Linux with XMonad and was tested again a few months ago, when it still booted successfully.

A workstation, not just a PC

Cthulhu is the modern working end of this environment: an Arch Linux computer used for daily computing, software development, website and Computer Museum work, World Observer work, and Linux gaming. Its AMD Ryzen 7 5800X3D processor, Radeon RX 9060 XT with 16 GB of graphics memory, 32 GB of RAM, and 3440 × 1440 ultrawide display describe a machine designed to do many kinds of work without changing its role.

Arch Linux supplies the operating system; the sway window manager arranges the desktop around keyboard-driven workflows. Package management keeps software installable and updatable as a coordinated system rather than as a shelf of physical boxes.

1998 PC

Usually built around one processor core, modest memory, local installation media and a desktop designed for a single machine. Networking was valuable, but not assumed to be part of every task.

2026 workstation

Many processor cores, a capable GPU, abundant memory and persistent networking let one computer be a creative workstation, development platform, game machine and client of other services at once.

Architecture changed more than speed

Multi-core processors allow separate tasks to make progress together. GPUs have become programmable, high-throughput companions to the processor. Linux offers many desktop environments and window managers, so the interface can be adapted to a workflow. Modern tools also assume fast networks: code, documentation, services and collaborators may be elsewhere even when the work is local.
